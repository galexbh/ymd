## Appearance

In **Settings → Appearance**, with a live **Preview** of the ledger:

- **Theme**: System, Light or Dark.
- **Accent color**: violet (default), indigo, teal, green, ochre, rust, magenta, graphite or a **Custom** one as `#rrggbb`. If the chosen color lacks contrast, ymd adjusts the shade so it stays readable. **Back to violet** restores the default.
- **Density**: Comfortable or Compact.
- **Corners**: Square, Soft or Rounded.
- **Text size**: applies to the whole interface.

## Language

In **Settings → Language**: **Follow system**, **Español** or **English**. The change applies immediately across the app.
