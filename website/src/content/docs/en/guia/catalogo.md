---
title: Catalog
description: The history of everything ymd has archived.
---

The **Catalog** screen keeps everything ymd has archived, with its date, title, preset, size and location. Unlike the [Ledger](/ymd/en/guia/descargar/#the-ledger), which shows the current session, the catalog persists across sessions.

## Search

**Search the catalog** finds entries by title, link, folder or accession number (**No.**). The **Type** filter narrows to video or audio. The catalog is paged.

## Actions

For each entry:

- **Open file** or **Show in folder**.
- **Download again**, with the same link.
- **Delete from catalog**: removes the entry, not the file.

If you moved or deleted the file outside ymd, the entry says **File moved or deleted**.

**Empty catalog** deletes every entry. Downloaded files stay in your folders.
